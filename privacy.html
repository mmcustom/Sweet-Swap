Privacy Policy — Sweet Swap

Last updated: September 2026

Sweet Swap is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains, plainly, what happens with your information when you play.

No personal data is collected

Sweet Swap does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, device identifiers, or usage information. The game does not require an account, does not have a backend server, and does not use analytics or tracking of any kind.

What is stored on your device

Your level progress and star ratings are saved locally on your device using browser local storage, purely so the game remembers where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes it.

Third-party resources

To display the game, the app loads the Phaser game engine from cdnjs.cloudflare.com and typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading these standard web resources means Cloudflare and Google may receive routine request information (such as your IP address) as part of serving those files, the same as any website that uses a CDN or web font. We do not receive, control, or use any of that information ourselves. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy for details on how they handle it.

No advertising

The game contains no advertisements and no ad networks are integrated into it.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds features that do collect data — this page will be updated to reflect that before the update ships.

Contact
